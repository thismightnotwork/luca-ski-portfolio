About Me

I'm a competitive alpine ski racer and qualified ski instructor based in the UK. I race for Hemel Ski Race Club (HSRC), teach at The Snow Centre (Hemel) and Knockhatch Adventure Park, and compete as part of the Snow Shepard Pro Team.

I hold BASI Ski UK Level 2 certification and focus primarily on slalom (SL) and giant slalom (GS).
