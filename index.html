Profile

Racing, coaching
and progression.

I’m a British U18 alpine ski racer and BASI Ski UK Level 2 instructor based in the UK. I train and race at The Snow Centre and Snozone, with mountain-racing experience in Tignes.

"Racing, coaching and progression."

I race for Hemel Ski Race Club (HSRC) and teach skiing at The Snow Centre in Hemel Hempstead and Knockhatch Adventure Park. My training combines dry-slope and snow-based race preparation in the UK with mountain-racing mileage in Tignes.

I’m on a Snow Shepherd Pro Deal, which gives me 50% off Snow Shepherd products. I’m always looking for support from brands, businesses and people who want to be part of my racing journey.
