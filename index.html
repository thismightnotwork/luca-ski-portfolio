Profile

Racing, coaching and progression.

I am a UK-based alpine skier whose skiing centres on performance, coaching and continual progression. I race with Hemel Ski Race Club (HSRC), teach skiing at The Snow Centre in Hemel Hempstead and Knockhatch Adventure Park, and represent Snow Shepard as part of its Pro Team.

On snow and on slope

Everything skiing.

Alpine racing

I race for Hemel Ski Race Club, focusing on slalom and giant slalom. Training is about technical consistency, speed through gates and carrying that work from dry slope to snow.

Ski instruction

As a BASI Ski UK Level 2 instructor, I teach at The Snow Centre, Hemel and Knockhatch Adventure Park, helping skiers build confidence, technique and a lasting enjoyment of the sport.

Snow Shepard Pro Team

I represent Snow Shepard through training, racing and skiing content, sharing the work behind progression as well as the mountain days that make it worthwhile.
