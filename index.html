British U18 alpine ski racer and BASI Ski UK Level 2 instructor based in the UK. Training and racing across The Snow Centre and Snozone, with mountain racing experience in Tignes.

"Precision in every turn. Intent in every line."

I race for Hemel Ski Race Club and instruct at The Snow Centre Hemel Hempstead and Knockhatch Adventure Park. My approach blends race technique with coaching fundamentals to build confidence and speed.

On a Snow Shepherd Pro Deal receiving 50% off Snow Shepherd products. Always open to partnerships with brands and businesses who want to be part of the racing journey.
