# Luca Finnis-Bernard

Luca Finnis-Bernard is a Great Britain U18 alpine ski racer, BASI Ski UK Level 2 instructor, and SnowShepherd Pro Team athlete. He races slalom and giant slalom for Hemel Ski Race Club.

## Profile

Luca is based in the UK. He trains and races at The Snow Centre and Snozone, with mountain-racing experience in Tignes.

He teaches skiing at The Snow Centre in Hemel Hempstead and Knockhatch Adventure Park. His training combines dry-slope and snow-based race preparation in the UK with mountain-racing mileage in Tignes.

## Race record
