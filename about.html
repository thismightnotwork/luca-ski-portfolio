About

Luca Finnis-Bernard

Luca Finnis-Bernard is a Great Britain U18 alpine ski racer, BASI Ski UK Level 2 ski instructor and SnowShepard Pro Team athlete. He races for Hemel Ski Race Club (HSRC), specialising in slalom and giant slalom.

Luca teaches skiing at The Snow Centre in Hemel Hempstead and Knockhatch Adventure Park. His training includes dry-slope and snow-based race training in the United Kingdom, together with mountain-racing experience in Tignes.

Race highlights

Luca has 11 recorded races, including 1 win, 7 podiums, and 11 top-10 finishes. His record includes 8 slalom races and 3 giant slalom races.

2025/26 activity

21 days on snow, 13 training sessions, 1 camp, 1 dry-training session, 2 countries, and 13 competitions.
