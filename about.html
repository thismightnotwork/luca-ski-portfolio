Official profile

Luca Finnis-Bernard

Great Britain U18 alpine ski racer, BASI Ski UK Level 2 instructor and SnowShepherd Pro Team athlete racing slalom and giant slalom for Hemel Ski Race Club.

Profile

Luca Finnis-Bernard is based in the UK. He trains and races at The Snow Centre and Snozone, with mountain-racing experience in Tignes.

He teaches skiing at The Snow Centre in Hemel Hempstead and Knockhatch Adventure Park. His training combines dry-slope and snow-based race preparation in the UK with mountain-racing mileage in Tignes.

Racing

  • Category: Great Britain U18.
  • Disciplines: slalom and giant slalom.
  • Race club: Hemel Ski Race Club.
  • Team: SnowShepherd Pro Team.
  • Qualification: BASI Ski UK Level 2.

The recorded race record contains 11 races, with 1 win, 7 podiums, 11 top-ten finishes, 8 slalom starts and 3 giant slalom starts.
